📖 Chapter 1
📚

School Days

I did not love English class at school.

But I loved movies, music, and games in English.

Slowly, English became fun — not just homework.

💻 Chapter 2
💻

Old Habits

As a developer, English is everywhere: code, docs, error messages.

I used to translate every word. It was slow and tiring.

I would stay up late, reading tutorials in English — again and again.

One day, I just stopped translating. I understood.

✈️ Chapter 3
✈️

The Decision

Why did I decide to come to Canada? Not just one reason.

Work must have been a big part of it.

But something else must have pushed me too.

It might have been a slow decision — built over many years.

🗣️ Chapter 4
🗣️

What People Said

Their words stayed with me.

Manager: "This is a great opportunity."
→ My manager said it was a great opportunity.
Friend: "You will love Vancouver."
→ My friend told me I would love Vancouver.
Mom: "Are you sure about this?"
→ My mom asked me if I was sure.

My Progress

My listening improved a lot here in Vancouver.

My confidence is growing — I'm not afraid to speak anymore.

English is not a school subject. It's a tool I use every day.
